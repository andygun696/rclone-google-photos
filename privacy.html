Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Purpose of the application

This is a personal, single-user integration using the open-source rclone software to upload the owner's photos and videos to the owner's Google Photos account.

Data access and use

The application accesses Google Photos only with the authorization and instructions of its owner. It is used to upload personal photos and videos. It does not operate as a service for other users.

Data storage and sharing

This application does not operate its own server or maintain a separate database of users' personal information. Photos and videos are transferred to Google Photos using rclone. Data is not sold or shared with third parties by the application.

Third-party services

Google Photos and Google account services are subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms. Rclone is open-source software.

Contact

This application is intended solely for personal use by its owner. For privacy-related questions, contact the application owner through the associated Google account.
